3. Quality and Relevance Improvements
A. Ground the 6G claims or remove them
The abstract and introduction mention 6G standardization, but every technical detail is about 5G SA. Unless you are explicitly testing a 6G-ready feature (e.g., AI/ML orchestration, sub-THz, or semantic communication), this looks speculative.
Suggestion: Replace "evolution towards 6G standardization" with "evolution towards advanced 5G and beyond-5G research", or justify exactly which 6G requirement (e.g., massive connectivity, network slicing) your testbed addresses.
B. Strengthen the actual comparison
Right now you compare installation effort and troubleshooting. That is valid, but shallow. Add one comparative dimension with numbers, even if simple:

C. Clarify the "friction points" contribution
Your best unique content is the troubleshooting of GTP-U routing, slice identity alignment, and PHY timing. Lead with this. In the Introduction, state: "Unlike prior work that treats deployment as a black box, we document the specific kernel-level and configuration-level fixes required to stabilize these stacks." This is a legitimate and useful contribution.
D. Fix the OAI / FlexRIC / xApps claim
Section 3.2 mentions FlexRIC and xApps/rApps as an OAI advantage, but you do not demonstrate or configure them. This is speculative. Either remove it or add a sentence: "While OAI's codebase supports FlexRIC integration, its evaluation is left for future work."
