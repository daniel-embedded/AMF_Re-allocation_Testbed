Back'n'forth consistency (four complete cycles: AMF1 → AMF2 → AMF1 → AMF2 → AMF1. It reboots all UEs simultaneously in each cycle since it uses run_concurrent):

~/docker-open5gs$ python3 sctp_proxy/amf_orchestrator_v7_dualproxy.py \
  --command backforth \
  --target amf2 \
  --wait-before 10 \
  --wait-after 60 \
  --cooldown 30 \
  --repeat 4 \
  --basename amf_realloc_backforth \
  --ue-pool ue1,ue2,ue3,ue4,ue5,ue6 \
  --gnb-pool gnb1

Partial re-allocation (it switches to AMF2 but reboots only three UEs and keeps three UEs connected to AMF1 through dead SCTP association):

~/docker-open5gs$ python3 sctp_proxy/amf_orchestrator_v7.py \
  --command partial \
  --target amf2 \
  --wait-before 10 \
  --wait-after 60 \
  --ue-subset ue1,ue2,ue3 \
  --basename amf_realloc_partial \
  --ue-pool ue1,ue2,ue3,ue4,ue5,ue6 \
  --gnb-pool gnb1

Isolate-gNB (it switches to AMF2 e reboot only gNB1. The UEs continue running. gNB1 loses the SCTP association with the proxy, restarts, and reconnects to the proxy → AMF2. The UEs must detect the signal loss and re-register):

~/docker-open5gs$ python3 sctp_proxy/amf_orchestrator_v7.py \
  --command isolate-gnb \
  --target amf2 \
  --wait-before 10 \
  --wait-after 60 \
  --basename amf_realloc_isolate_gnb \
  --ue-pool ue1,ue2,ue3,ue4,ue5,ue6 \
  --gnb-pool gnb1
